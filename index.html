About

01

Who we are

Siliconia Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is an ISO-certified technology company specializing in advanced radar systems, space technologies, satellite communications and unmanned aerial solutions for defence and aerospace applications.

Our pursuit of excellence has earned us national recognition, including two prestigious IDEX Challenge awards for our pioneering work in AESA Radar and Satellite Communication Antenna technologies. These accolades reflect our unwavering commitment to pushing technological boundaries and delivering mission-critical solutions that empower defence, aerospace, and allied sectors in an ever-evolving global landscape.

02

Our vision

At Siliconia Technologies, our vision is to shape the future of advanced technologies by delivering smart, secure and transformative solutions.

We are committed to driving innovation and excellence in a rapidly evolving world, continually pushing the boundaries of what is possible. Through a culture of continuous learning, collaborative partnerships and a passionate team, we strive to solve complex challenges and create meaningful impact across industries.

This approach enables us to develop advanced, future-ready systems that address real-world needs. By collaborating with both defence and private sector partners, we focus on fostering indigenization, driving innovation and ensuring seamless integration across our solutions. Our work is aimed at contributing to a safer, smarter and more connected world through practical and meaningful technological advancements.

Recognition

Recognised by DPIIT

Siliconia Technologies is proudly recognized as a startup in the semiconductor sector by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Government of India.

We have successfully won two prestigious R&D challenges under the Innovations for Defence Excellence (iDEX) initiative, demonstrating our commitment to cutting-edge innovation in defense technology.

Siliconia has also been showcased as a potential exporter of indigenous, high-end technology products and systems for marine and aviation applications—serving both civil and government sectors.
